Subscriptions

  • Passla Premium, monthly or yearly, is an auto-renewing subscription. The price is shown in the app before you buy.
  • The yearly plan starts with a 3-day free trial for eligible users; the monthly plan has no trial. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the yearly subscription starts and is charged to your Apple Account.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. A subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; the renewal is charged within the 24 hours before that end.
  • Manage or cancel in your Apple Account settings: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Any unused part of a free trial ends when you buy a subscription.
  • The free plan keeps up to 50 passwords, with the generator, authenticator, password health and AutoFill. Premium removes the limit and adds secure notes.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple, under its policies: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your vault, your keys

Passla cannot read your vault and cannot recover it. If you set a master password and forget it, or lose your device with neither iCloud Keychain nor an encrypted backup file of your own, we cannot restore your data — there is no copy on our side. Keep your device secured.
