My subscription is missing on a new iPhone

Open Premium in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Passla. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next renewal.

I forgot my master password

Passla never sees it and cannot reset it. That is what keeps the vault private, and it means a forgotten master password cannot be recovered by us.

Moving to a new iPhone

Set up the new iPhone from your iCloud backup, or with Quick Start, with Passwords and Keychain turned on in Settings → your name → iCloud: Passla opens the vault there by itself. If it says it can't open the vault yet, iCloud Keychain may still be syncing; wait a few minutes and tap Try again.

Without iCloud Keychain, use an encrypted backup file: Settings → Import / Export → Encrypted backup. It carries everything, passwords, 2FA accounts, notes and cards. You choose a password for the file, and that password opens it in Passla on any iPhone: on the new one, choose Select File to Import, pick the backup and type the password. Passla never sees the backup password and cannot recover it, so keep it somewhere other than your phone.

A plain CSV export moves your passwords too, but anyone who gets the file can read it. Delete it once imported.

Deleting my data

In Passla, use Settings → Reset Vault, then delete the app. The reset also removes the vault's key from iCloud Keychain; copies of the encrypted vault in your iPhone backups go when those backups do. Apple keeps purchase records as billing requires.

Why is the breach check off?

It is off by default and asks first. Turn it on in Settings → Privacy → Breach checking; only the first 5 characters of a password's hash are sent, never the password.
